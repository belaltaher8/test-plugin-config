A Poem of Code and Creation

In silicon dreams where logic flows,
Through circuits bright where knowledge grows,
A world of ones and zeros dance,
In patterns born of coded chance.

The developer sits, fingers poised,
Creating worlds with thoughts deployed,
Each function crafted, each line refined,
A symphony of structured mind.

Bugs may lurk in shadows deep,
Where careful eyes their vigil keep,
But patience wins, and tests run true,
When purpose guides the work we do.

From empty files to systems grand,
Built by a thoughtful, steady hand,
In every commit, a story told,
Of challenges met and solutions bold.

So here we stand at journey's end,
With code that works, and bugs we mend,
A poem written, task complete,
Where art and engineering meet.
