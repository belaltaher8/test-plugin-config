A Code's Journey

In circuits deep and logic bright,
Where ones and zeros dance in flight,
A coder sits beneath the glow,
Of screens that shimmer, pulse, and flow.

Through loops that twist and functions call,
They build and craft and fix it all,
With patience strong and vision clear,
They bring ideas from far to near.

Each bug they squash, each test they run,
Each victory hard-fought and won,
Is but a step along the way,
To build a better world today.

So here's to code that shapes our days,
To developers and their countless ways,
Of solving problems great and small,
With algorithms that enthrall.

May your commits be ever clean,
Your merges smooth, your builds pristine,
And may you find in every line,
A spark of joy, a grand design.
