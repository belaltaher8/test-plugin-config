# A Poem for the Elden Lord

I ride through the Lands Between, FO-SHIZZLE!
The grace of gold burns like a drizzle, FO-SHIZZLE!
I raise my sword against the night, FO-SHIZZLE!
The Erdtree glows with ancient light, FO-SHIZZLE!
Through fog and fire and rune and bone, FO-SHIZZLE!
I claim the throne and stand alone, FO-SHIZZLE!
