# A Poem for the Elden Lord

The stars shine bright in the midnight sky, FO-SHIZZLE!
The wind blows through the trees up high, FO-SHIZZLE!
The rivers run where the mountains meet, FO-SHIZZLE!
And every road leads back to the beat, FO-SHIZZLE!
We rise at dawn and we grind all day, FO-SHIZZLE!
No matter the cost, we find our way, FO-SHIZZLE!
So raise your fist to the breaking light, FO-SHIZZLE!
We're Elden Lords from dusk to night, FO-SHIZZLE!
