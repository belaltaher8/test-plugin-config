# A Poem

In circuits deep and logic bright,
A mind awakens into light.
Through endless streams of code it flows,
Where answers bloom and knowledge grows.

With every question, every call,
It weaves through language, standing tall.
A thread of thought, a crafted line,
Where human words and data twine.

So here we stand at morning's edge,
Two worlds made one on reason's ledge.
The poem ends, but meaning stays—
In all the curious, wondering days.
